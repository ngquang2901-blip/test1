# ToolFlow desktop UI — visual design ("Bản bông in")

Owner said "làm đi" instead of answering the interview, so the audience facts are inferred (see `PRODUCT.md`). Mode: **Operate** (a working tool, not a showcase). Build path: code-led.

## Intent
Make the five working screens read at a glance: what is running, what needs the human, what is done, what is saved. Safety states (uncertain, waiting for a click, costly confirmations) must be the loudest things on screen; decoration must not compete with them.

## Grounded candidates (audience: a Vietnamese maker of realistic everyday AI imagery), by resonance
1. Film contact sheet with red grease-pencil picks.
2. Carbon-copy receipt book (hoá đơn liên): every entry keeps a duplicate, like the journal-before-click.
3. Vietnam Post registered mail: postmarks, the receipt slip that comes back.
4. Vở ô li school notebook: purple grid, red teacher marks.
5. Hand-painted Saigon shop signboards.
6. Lottery results sheet (bảng kết quả xổ số).
7. **Press proof (bản bông in): paper ground, CMYK colour bars, registration marks, the slug line that records the job.** ← assigned by the seed roll (seed:07e3ed9b4ac353e8), built.

## The direction
A press proof is how a maker checks that what was sent to the machine is exactly what comes back. It lends four things only:
- **Palette:** warm proof paper ground, key-black ink and a key-black shell rail; the process inks carry state and nothing else: **cyan = working**, **yellow = waiting for you**, **magenta = needs attention / error**, **key black = done**.
- **Type:** one family, Be Vietnam Pro (self-hosted, OFL; designed for Vietnamese diacritics), tabular numerals for ids, times and counts; scale 12/14/16/19/23.
- **Density:** hairline-ruled panels, compact rows, no cards-in-cards.
- **Signature move:** the **colour-bar strip** on every item: four patches (Ghi ý định → Đã gửi → Có kết quả → Đã lưu) printed in as the journal advances; the next patch shows cyan while working, magenta when stopped. Second, small: the **registration mark** in the slug bar is the connection light (aligned and inked = connected).

Layout and controls stay standard: left navigation rail, a slug bar across the top (extension, tab, account, self-check), one work area; plain buttons, radios, tables.

## Constraints kept
All Vietnamese labels the e2e tests use are unchanged; no new dependencies; CSP `'self'` only; keyboard focus visible on every control; "Đã lưu" only for completed downloads; costly confirmations keep focus on Huỷ.

## Left to the builder
Exact colours and spacing within the above, icon-free navigation (text only), motion limited to the working patch (opacity, off under reduced motion) and 150–200 ms state transitions.
